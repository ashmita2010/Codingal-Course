About Codingal

A little bit about me

Codingal is on a mission to inspire school kids to fall in love with coding. Codingal started with a dream to inspire school kids to fall in love with coding and to empower every child to build anything they can imagine. They will be creators of the future.


We encourage children to ask as many questions as possible. We believe a curious mind will do wonders.We want to make learning computer science a fun activity children look forward to. We are constantly working to mke our sessions and activities as interactive and entertaining for the kids as possible.
